Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TheUrbanNet LTD (“TheUrbanNet”, “the Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our platform.

We are committed to protecting your personal data and handling it transparently, fairly, and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office.

By using TheUrbanNet, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

About TheUrbanNet

TheUrbanNet is a digital platform that enables users to discover and connect with independent service providers.

TheUrbanNet operates solely as a technology and discovery platform. We do not provide, manage, or deliver the services listed by independent service providers.

For the purposes of data protection law, TheUrbanNet LTD is the data controller for personal data collected through the platform.

Independent service providers may act as separate and independent data controllers in relation to personal data they collect or receive outside of TheUrbanNet.

We collect and process different categories of personal data depending on how you interact with the platform.

Profile Information

  • Platform interactions and usage data

Cookies and Tracking Data

We may collect information through cookies and similar technologies. More information is available in our Cookie Policy .

3. Information from Third Parties

We may receive limited personal data from third-party services, such as:

Communication

Lawful Basis for Processing

We rely on the following lawful bases under the UK GDPR:

  • Contractual necessity: To provide the platform and its services.
  • Legitimate interests: To operate, improve, and secure the platform.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is required by law.
  • Consent: Where applicable, including cookies and marketing communications.

Messaging and Platform Boundaries

TheUrbanNet provides messaging functionality to allow users to contact independent service providers with enquiries.

Important clarifications include:

  • Messaging is provided for initial communication only.
  • TheUrbanNet does not currently facilitate bookings, payments, or transactions.
  • TheUrbanNet does not monitor or control communications that take place outside the platform.
  • Once a user chooses to engage with a service provider outside the platform, that provider may act as an independent data controller.

TheUrbanNet is not responsible for how independent service providers collect, use, store, or process personal data outside the platform.

With Service Providers and Processors

Legal and Regulatory Disclosure

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the platform and its features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the purpose for which it is used. Where personal data is no longer required, we may delete or anonymise it.

International Data Transfers

In some cases, personal data may be transferred to or processed in countries outside the United Kingdom.

Where this occurs, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable law. These safeguards may include adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or other recognised legal mechanisms.

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

These measures include the use of secure systems, access controls, and encryption where appropriate. However, no system can be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have a number of rights in relation to your personal data. Depending on the circumstances, these may include the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Request deletion of your personal data

  • Object to certain types of processing

You can exercise these rights by contacting us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your personal data has been handled unlawfully.
