✦ FAQs

How Early Access Works

Joining the waitlist is simple. Choose how you plan to use TheUrbanNet, tell us what matters to you, and be among the first to hear when TheUrbanNet launches.

What happens after I join the waitlist

Once you sign up, we’ll send a short guide to your inbox with more about TheUrbanNet, what’s coming, and what you can expect. You’ll also receive important updates as we get closer to launch.

Can customers and service providers both join?

Yes. Customers can tell us which services they are interested in, while service providers can share more about their business and the services they offer. Just choose the option that best describes you.

Is it free to join the waitlist?

Yes, joining the waitlist is completely free. There is no payment or commitment required. It simply lets us know you’re interested and keeps you connected to future updates.

How will my information be used?

We’ll use your responses to understand what customers and service providers need from TheUrbanNet. Your details will also be used to send relevant waitlist, early-access, and launch updates.
