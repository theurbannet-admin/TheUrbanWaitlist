Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how TheUrbanNet LTD (“TheUrbanNet”, “the Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you access or use our platform, website, or related services.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we use personal data more broadly.

We are committed to complying with applicable UK laws, including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and guidance issued by the Information Commissioner's Office.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device, such as a computer, smartphone, or tablet, when you visit a website.

They allow the website to:

  • Recognise your device
  • Store preferences or actions
  • Enable certain functionality
  • Collect information about how the website is used

Cookies may be set by TheUrbanNet directly, known as first-party cookies, or by other organisations, known as third-party cookies.

Types of Cookies We Use

TheUrbanNet uses several categories of cookies, each serving different purposes.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the platform.

They enable core functionality such as:

  • Account login and authentication
  • Session management
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Network management

Without these cookies, the platform cannot function properly. These cookies do not require user consent under PECR.

Performance and Analytical Cookies

These cookies collect information about how users interact with TheUrbanNet.

They help us understand:

  • Which pages are visited
  • How users navigate the platform
  • How long users spend on pages
  • Whether errors occur

This data is typically aggregated and anonymised.

We use this information to:

  • Improve platform performance
  • Optimise the user experience
  • Identify technical issues

These cookies are only used where you have provided consent.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow TheUrbanNet to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality.

They may store:

  • Language preferences
  • Saved settings
  • Previously viewed content
  • User interface customisation

These cookies improve usability but are not strictly necessary. They are used based on your consent.

Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies track browsing behaviour to deliver relevant content or advertisements.

They may be used to:

  • Measure the effectiveness of campaigns
  • Limit how often you see content
  • Personalise content based on interests

These cookies may be set by third-party advertising partners.

TheUrbanNet does not currently prioritise advertising functionality. If it is introduced, these cookies will only be used with your consent.

Social Media Cookies

These cookies are set by social media platforms when content is shared or embedded.

They may allow:

  • Content to be shared directly from TheUrbanNet
  • Interaction with social media features
  • Engagement tracking

These cookies are controlled by the relevant third-party platforms.

Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as:

  • Pixels: Small images used to track activity.
  • Local storage: Data stored directly in your browser.
  • Session storage: Temporary storage used during a browsing session.

These technologies perform similar functions to cookies and may be used for analytics, functionality, and performance purposes.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Ensure the platform operates securely and effectively
  • Enable login and account functionality
  • Remember user preferences
  • Analyse platform usage
  • Improve features and performance
  • Maintain platform integrity and prevent misuse

Cookies do not allow us to access your device beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party providers that support TheUrbanNet, such as:

  • Analytics services
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Security monitoring tools
  • Embedded content providers

These third parties may collect data directly from your browser.

We do not control how third parties use this data. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Consent and Control

When you first visit TheUrbanNet, you will be presented with a cookie banner requesting your consent to use non-essential cookies.

You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customise your preferences

Strictly necessary cookies will always remain active.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time through Cookie Settings.

Managing Cookies

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Receive alerts before cookies are stored

However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of TheUrbanNet.

Data Protection

Where cookies collect personal data, that data will be processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy .

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or platform functionality. We will notify users where applicable.
